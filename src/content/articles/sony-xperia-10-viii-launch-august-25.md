Sony is scheduled to hold an Xperia launch event on August 25, where it is expected to unveil the**Sony Xperia 10 VIII**. YugaTech reports that the mid-range smartphone has appeared on Geekbench under model number XQ-GH54, with reported single-core and multi-core scores of 871 and 3,028. The handset is expected to use Qualcomm’s Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 chipset, the same platform reported for its predecessor, while early reports suggest a more angular design.

The launch event is scheduled for 11:00 AM Japan Standard Time, when Sony is expected to share the phone’s complete specifications, pricing, and market availability. Philippine buyers should wait for local confirmation before assuming that the device will be sold in the country. For now, the announcement provides an early look at Sony’s next mid-range Xperia model and its possible focus on familiar performance hardware with a refreshed exterior.
