VST ECS Phils. has added Seagate SkyHawk AI and IronWolf Pro hard drives to its Philippine storage portfolio, widening the options available to businesses building video analytics, network-attached storage and enterprise server systems. The companies marked the expanded engagement at the VST ECS CXO Tech Summit 2026 in Boracay. The addition puts two workload-specific Seagate lines beside the Exos enterprise drives VST ECS already distributes, giving local partners a broader storage conversation as companies collect more video, transactions and machine-generated data.


![Seagate SkyHawk and IronWolf storage products](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/vst-ecs-seagate-storage-philippines/image-1.webp?v=2026112106000)

SkyHawk AI is aimed at systems that record and analyze video continuously. Seagate says the line can support up to 64 high-definition cameras and 32 additional AI streams, a combination intended for deployments that use cameras as data sources rather than simple security feeds. That distinction matters for Philippine malls, offices, warehouses and schools adding analytics to existing surveillance infrastructure. Storage planning must account for retention periods, camera resolution and model-generated metadata, not only the number of cameras connected to a network.


![Seagate hard drives for video and NAS workloads](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/vst-ecs-seagate-storage-philippines/image-2.webp?v=2026112106000)

IronWolf Pro serves a different but related need. The line is designed for commercial and enterprise RAID storage of up to 24 bays, with workloads of up to 550TB per year. Both product families reach capacities of up to 32TB, are built for 24/7 operation and come with a five-year limited warranty plus three years of Rescue Data Recovery Services. Those specifications do not remove the need for backups or tested recovery plans, but they give resellers a clearer way to match hardware to sustained business workloads.


![VST ECS and Seagate distribution partnership](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/vst-ecs-seagate-storage-philippines/image-3.webp?v=2026112106000)

The portfolio expansion also reflects how storage is becoming part of an organization’s AI strategy. Video systems generate the data used by analytics, while NAS and enterprise arrays hold the files that teams need to retrieve, share and protect. VST ECS said the new lines are available through its authorized resellers nationwide. Philippine buyers should still compare total cost, redundancy, network performance and recovery procedures before choosing a drive. The practical lesson is simple: AI tools are only as useful as the data infrastructure that supports them.
