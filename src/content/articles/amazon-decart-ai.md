Amazon announced an agreement to purchase the proprietary technology behind Decart’s Optimization Stack and bring the startup’s co-founders and AI research team into Amazon. The technology is designed to improve how AI workloads run across hardware, with a focus on inference performance and custom silicon. Amazon said the deal builds on work that began in 2025, when the two companies collaborated on performance results for Trainium. The transaction is significant because AI progress increasingly depends not only on larger models, but also on extracting more useful work from every chip.

![Decart logo representing the AI optimization technology joining Amazon](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/amazon-decart-ai/image-1.webp?v=202611240918)

Decart’s Optimization Stack takes a full-stack approach, connecting software techniques with the behavior of the underlying hardware. Amazon said that approach can help customers train and run models faster and at lower cost. The company is also adding Decart co-founders Dean Leitersdorf, Moshe Shalev, and Orian Leitersdorf, along with their researchers and engineers. The announcement describes a technology and talent transaction rather than a conventional consumer product launch. Its immediate impact will be felt inside Amazon’s AI infrastructure work, especially where software efficiency affects capacity and operating costs.

![Amazon sign representing the cloud and custom-silicon infrastructure behind the deal](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/amazon-decart-ai/image-2.webp?v=202611240918)

Amazon’s custom silicon portfolio includes Trainium for AI training and inference, alongside Graviton processors and other infrastructure systems. The company said Decart’s expertise can help optimize inference, reinforcement learning, multimodal systems, agentic workloads, and world models. That list shows why performance software has become strategically important. AI providers need more throughput and lower cost, but they also need hardware to support changing model designs. Better optimization can delay some capacity pressure, although it cannot remove the need for chips, data-center power, memory, and network infrastructure.

![Amazon founder Jeff Bezos beside the company logo, representing Amazon’s technology strategy](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/amazon-decart-ai/image-3.webp?v=202611240918)

The agreement is not yet complete. Amazon said closing remains subject to customary conditions, including regulatory clearance, and warned that the expected benefits are forward-looking. The company still has to integrate the technology, retain the incoming team, and show that projected gains translate into reliable customer workloads. For the wider AI market, the deal reinforces a clear trend: optimization software is becoming as important as model development because every improvement in utilization changes the economics of deployment. The next proof point will be measurable performance on Amazon’s production systems.
