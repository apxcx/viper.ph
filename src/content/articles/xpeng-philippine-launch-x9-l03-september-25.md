XPeng is scheduled to make its official Philippine brand debut on September 25 at the**SM Mall of Asia Arena**in Pasay City, bringing the X9 and L03 into the local electric-vehicle conversation. AutoIndustriya reported that the “Drive into a New Day” event will combine vehicle displays with showcases of XPeng’s Physical AI work. The X2 electric flying car and IRON humanoid robot are also expected to appear. The event is a market launch and technology showcase, not proof that every displayed product is already available for Philippine purchase.

The first local lineup centers on the**XPeng X9 Ultra Smart seven-seat MPV**and the L03 next-generation AI SUV coupe. AutoIndustriya reported suggested pricing of P4.058 million for the X9, with a P25,000 reservation fee, and P2.058 million for the L03, with a P10,000 reservation fee. The company is expected to present final variants, delivery plans, warranty coverage and promotions at the event. Buyers should treat those details as launch information until XPeng publishes final local terms for each trim.

XPeng’s official L03 page describes a cabin and driver-assistance platform powered by self-developed**Turing chips**, with pure-electric and extended-range powertrain options depending on market and configuration. The page also warns that specifications, features and visuals can vary by trim and market. The X9’s local presentation is expected to emphasize rear-wheel steering, flexible seating and intelligent vehicle systems. Actual figures depend on the final local trim. These are technology and product claims from the company and launch reporting, not independent performance testing under Philippine road or charging conditions.

XPeng Philippines has reported an initial dealership footprint in Greenhills, Makati, North EDSA and Cebu, while the launch requires advance registration and email confirmation for free admission. The appearance of the X2 and IRON is intended to illustrate the company’s broader Physical AI direction; it should not be reported as commercial Philippine availability of a flying car or humanoid robot. Viper.ph will update the story after the event with confirmed local pricing, warranty terms, delivery timing and any changes to the initial model lineup.
