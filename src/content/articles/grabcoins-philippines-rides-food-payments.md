Grab Philippines is expanding**GrabCoins Philippines**rewards across more parts of its ecosystem, including rides, food orders, grocery purchases, and cashless payments. YugaTech reports that users can earn coins through eligible GrabCar bookings, GrabFood and GrabMart orders, GrabPay transactions, and selected in-app activities. The wider rewards program gives customers more ways to collect and redeem points while using services they already rely on for transportation, delivery, and everyday purchases.

GrabUnlimited members can earn six GrabCoins for every PHP 100 spent through GrabPay and three coins for every PHP 100 spent through other eligible cashless methods. Grab is also introducing Double Coins Days from the 23rd to the 25th of each month. Coins can be exchanged for vouchers or applied to eligible GrabCar, GrabFood, GrabMart, and supported QR Ph transactions, and remain valid for six months after earning.
