Mynt Inc., the fintech company behind GCash, has set its initial public offering price at**₱6.60 per share**, according to October 2 reports from ABS-CBN News and BusinessWorld. The shares are scheduled to list on the Philippine Stock Exchange Main Board under the ticker GCASH on October 20, 2026. The pricing announcement brings the planned debut closer, but it does not mean trading has started. Investors still need to follow the final prospectus, exchange notices and participating broker or platform instructions before considering any subscription.

BusinessWorld, citing a Philippine Stock Exchange notice, reported an offering of up to 8.03 billion common shares. That total separates up to 1.61 billion newly issued primary shares from up to 6.42 billion existing secondary shares, with an overallotment option for up to 1.20 billion additional secondary shares. At ₱6.60, BusinessWorld calculated a base offering of about ₱53 billion and roughly ₱60.92 billion if the overallotment is fully exercised. Those are offer-value calculations, not guaranteed net proceeds or a valuation conclusion.

GMA News reported that 100 shares would cost ₱660 before applicable fees and charges, while qualified Local Small Investors may use PSE EASy. Reports also said shares were expected to be available through GStocks in the GCash app. Philstar reported a public-offer window of October 6 to 12, but that timing should be checked against Mynt’s final prospectus and PSE notice. The ₱660 example is illustrative, not a minimum subscription requirement, and the reported allocations and cornerstone commitments remain subject to final conditions.

The listing would give the Philippine market a major fintech flotation and a new way for eligible investors to access the GCash parent, but this article makes no claim about returns, valuation or suitability. Readers should distinguish the final offer price from the first trading price, which may move after listing. They should also verify account eligibility, fees, order cutoffs and allocation rules with authorized channels. The confirmed news is Mynt’s ₱6.60 pricing and planned October 20 debut; several operational details remain prospectus-dependent.
