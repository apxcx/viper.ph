The Philippine Statistics Authority is expanding the practical use of the National ID in financial technology through new integrations with Salmon Bank, ARX, and Island Credit Solution. The partnerships use National ID Authentication Services, or NIDAS, to verify identities and streamline customer onboarding. The move shifts the National ID from a registration document toward a reusable trust layer. For customers, that could mean fewer repeated verification steps when opening accounts, accessing credit information, or using business platforms that must confirm who is on the other side of a transaction.


![Philippine Statistics Authority and technology representatives holding signed partnership documents](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/psa-national-id-fintech-authentication/image-1.webp?v=202611130834)

PSA said Salmon Bank and ARX signed subscription contracts at its Quezon City headquarters on October 13, while Island Credit Solution integrated NIDAS earlier into its CreditMo platform. CreditMo users can access credit reports, scores, and history in real time after identity verification. Salmon Bank is using NIDAS to support Know Your Customer checks and account opening in its app. ARX Pay, meanwhile, supports real-time verification for business clients that include regulators and gaming operators, extending the service beyond consumer banking.


![Speaker presenting National ID authentication information at a Philippine Statistics Authority event](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/psa-national-id-fintech-authentication/image-2.webp?v=202611130834)

The scale is significant. PSA reported 94,438,067 Filipinos registered with the National ID and 44 million authentications in the financial sector as of August 31. It said the partnerships align with a Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas memorandum issued in August that requires banks and other financial institutions to use NIDAS for customer verification. The covered activities include account opening, onboarding, and record updates. Those requirements connect digital identity adoption with data protection, cybersecurity, anti-money-laundering controls, and consumer trust.


![Graphic comparing offline National ID verification with online authentication levels](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/psa-national-id-fintech-authentication/image-3.webp?v=202611130834)

The next test is dependable service without weakening privacy. PSA officials said the agency will keep onboarding financial institutions and expanding use cases, while partners emphasized fraud prevention and secure access. That balance will matter as more services rely on online identity checks. A fast verification step is useful only when people understand how their data is handled, institutions protect the resulting records, and authentication failures have a clear recovery path. NIDAS gives Philippine fintech companies a common rail, but responsible implementation will determine its value.
