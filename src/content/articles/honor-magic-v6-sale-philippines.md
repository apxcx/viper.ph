The**HONOR Magic V6**is now officially on sale nationwide in the Philippines, following its first-day event at the HONOR Store in SM North EDSA. Manila Standard reports that the flagship foldable is available through HONOR stores, partner dealers, and online channels, giving Filipino buyers wider access after the brand’s pre-order period.

The device features a 6,660mAh silicon-carbon battery, 80W wired and 66W wireless charging, a 3nm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 platform, and HONOR’s AI Falcon Camera System. IP68 and IP69 ratings, a reinforced hinge, and an anti-scratch shield add durability. Pre-order customers can also claim a free HONOR Pad X8b, subject to the offer terms. Source:[Manila Standard](https://manilastandard.net/tech/gadgets/314788605/the-wait-is-over-honor-magic-v6-now-officially-on-sale-nationwide.html).
