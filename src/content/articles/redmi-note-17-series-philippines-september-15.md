Xiaomi Philippines has confirmed that the**REDMI Note 17 Series**will launch locally on September 15, 2026. YugaTech reports that the company will hold a livestream at 5:00 PM Philippine time, with the REDMI Note 17 Pro Max as the headline model. The device is positioned for buyers who want a large battery and bright, fast display without moving immediately into the highest flagship price tier.

The REDMI Note 17 Pro Max is listed with a 6.83-inch 1.5K AMOLED panel, 120Hz refresh rate, Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 processor, and a 10,000mAh battery with 100W wired charging. Xiaomi has not yet confirmed which variants or local prices will arrive, so buyers should wait for the official launch before making comparisons. Source:[YugaTech](https://www.yugatech.com/news/redmi-note-17-series-philippine-launch-september-15/).
