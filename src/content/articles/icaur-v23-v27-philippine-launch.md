iCAUR Philippines will formally launch its local brand presence on November 24 and use the event to reveal final prices for the V23 BEV and V27 Golden REEV SUVs. The company has already previewed both models and appeared at the Auto Focus Multibrand Test Drive Festival, but the launch is designed to turn early interest into a clearer buying proposition. Guests will see standard vehicles, Pioneer Edition versions and customized builds, giving the new marque a chance to explain how its rugged, retro-influenced design fits different ownership plans in the Philippine market.

![iCAUR V23 and V27 displayed in Philippine pre-sale media](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/icaur-v23-v27-philippine-launch/image-1.webp?v=2026112208000)

The V23 is the fully electric model, with initial price indications of about P1.5 million for rear-wheel drive and P1.8 million for all-wheel drive. Those figures are estimates, not final suggested retail prices, and BusinessMirror says the official numbers will come at the November 24 launch. The compact SUV combines a boxy, classic look with battery-electric propulsion. For Philippine buyers, the eventual value calculation will include charging access, warranty coverage, service reach and the cost of living with an electric vehicle beyond the showroom.

![iCAUR V23 electric SUV side profile](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/icaur-v23-v27-philippine-launch/image-2.webp?v=2026112208000)

The larger V27 uses iCAUR’s Golden REEV setup. Electric motors drive the wheels, while a gasoline-powered generator helps extend usable range on longer trips. Indicative estimates put the rear-wheel-drive version near P2.1 million and the all-wheel-drive version near P2.5 million, again subject to the official announcement. That configuration gives the V27 a different pitch from the battery-only V23: it keeps electric drive at the center while offering a generator for journeys where public charging infrastructure is less predictable.

![iCAUR V23 and V27 SUV comparison](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/icaur-v23-v27-philippine-launch/image-3.webp?v=2026112208000)

The launch will also test whether iCAUR can support its products after the reveal. The company expects to begin first Pioneer Edition customer deliveries on November 25 and has identified dealership locations in Greenhills and Iloilo City. It is targeting 20 dealerships nationwide by 2027 under OMODA & JAECOO Motor Philippines. The first 200 qualifying customers for each model were offered Pioneer Edition benefits such as discounts, accessories and charging equipment. Final prices, delivery execution and aftersales coverage will determine how quickly curiosity becomes adoption.
