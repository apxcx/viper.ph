Lenovo has refreshed its compact Android gaming tablet with the**Legion Y700 AI**, built around an 8.4-inch 2.5K OLED display with a 165Hz variable refresh rate and up to 4,000 nits of local peak brightness. YugaTech reports that the tablet uses Qualcomm’s Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor, with configurations reaching 24GB of LPDDR5T RAM and 1TB of UFS 4.1 Pro storage. Its 6.5mm body weighs about 298 grams.

The tablet adds ZUI 16 features such as real-time AI performance scheduling, contextual search, active sensing, and voice translation. Other specifications include 5G dual-SIM support, Wi-Fi 7, dual USB-C ports, bypass charging, and a 7,470mAh battery with 68W charging. Lenovo has not yet announced Philippine pricing or availability, so local buyers will need to wait for official distribution details.
