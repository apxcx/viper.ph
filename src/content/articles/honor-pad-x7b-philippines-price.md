HONOR Philippines has brought the HONOR Pad X7b to local shoppers through TikTok Shop, giving the tablet an early-bird price of PHP8,999 from November 6 to 13. The offer cuts PHP2,000 from its PHP10,999 suggested retail price and arrives during the TikTok 11.11 Big Bang Sale. The tablet is positioned for entertainment, browsing, reading and everyday tasks rather than high-end creative production. Its launch gives budget-conscious Filipino buyers another large-screen option ahead of the device’s broader nationwide availability scheduled for November 28.

![Tablet computer with keyboard accessory](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/honor-pad-x7b-philippines-price/image-1.webp?v=202611070710)

The Pad X7b uses a 9.7-inch 2K HONOR Eye Comfort Display with a 90Hz refresh rate. That combination is aimed at smoother scrolling and more comfortable viewing for video, web pages and reading. HONOR also lists an 8,000mAh battery, which supports the product’s emphasis on extended use away from a charger. Display resolution alone does not determine the experience, however. App support, brightness, speaker quality and the tablet’s weight will matter just as much for students, families and commuters deciding whether a lower-cost slate fits their routine.

![Portable tablet PC display and stylus](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/honor-pad-x7b-philippines-price/image-2.webp?v=202611070710)

Durability is another part of the pitch. The Pad X7b carries SGS 5-star Enhanced Drop and Crush Resistance Certification, a claim that speaks to everyday handling rather than making the device indestructible. Buyers should still use a case and treat certification as one factor in a purchase decision. The tablet also supports 0% interest installments for up to six months through TikTok PayLater, subject to the platform’s terms. That financing option may broaden access, but shoppers should compare the final payable amount and delivery conditions against the cash promotion.

![Tablet device used for reading and productivity](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/honor-pad-x7b-philippines-price/image-3.webp?v=202611070710)

The early-bird offer makes timing central to the launch. Customers who shop between November 6 and 13 can access the discounted PHP8,999 price and livestream promotions from participating creators, while the official launch and nationwide availability follow later in the month. For Philippine consumers, the practical question is whether the Pad X7b’s screen, battery and durability package delivers enough value for school, entertainment or light productivity. The answer will depend on software performance and long-term support as much as the sale headline.
