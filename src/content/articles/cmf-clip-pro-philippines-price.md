The**CMF Clip Pro**open-ear earbuds are arriving in the Philippines on September 4 for PHP 5,990. Gadget Pilipinas reports that the product will be sold through Digital Walker, open_source by Beyond the Box, and Nothing PH channels. Its clip-style design is aimed at people who want to stay aware of their surroundings while listening during commutes, workdays, workouts, and other active routines.

The earbuds use 10.8mm dual-magnet drivers, Ultra Bass Technology, four high-definition microphones, and Clear Voice Technology. Battery life reaches up to 10 hours per charge or 32.5 hours with the case, while Bluetooth 5.4, dual-device connection, low-latency mode, and IP54 protection add practical features. Buyers should verify stock and warranty terms through authorized Philippine sellers. Source:[Gadget Pilipinas](https://www.gadgetpilipinas.net/2026/09/cmf-clip-pro-ph-price/).
