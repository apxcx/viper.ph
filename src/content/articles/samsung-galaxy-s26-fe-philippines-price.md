Samsung has officially launched the**Galaxy S26 FE**in the Philippines, adding a more affordable model to its current flagship family. YugaTech reports that the phone has a 6.7-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display with a 120Hz refresh rate, an Exynos 2500 chipset, a 50MP triple-camera system, and a 4,900mAh battery with 45W wired charging. It also ships with One UI 9 and new Galaxy AI features including My FanCam, Photo Assist, Gemini Omni, and an updated Now Brief.

Local pricing starts at PHP 46,990 for 8GB RAM with 128GB storage, rising to PHP 53,990 for 256GB and PHP 67,990 for 512GB. The phone is available at Samsung Experience Stores nationwide, with Samsung.com availability scheduled for September 11. Introductory discounts of up to PHP 7,000 and trade-in offers run until November 30, subject to Samsung’s terms. Source:[YugaTech](https://www.yugatech.com/news/samsung-galaxy-s26-fe-specs-price-philippines/).
