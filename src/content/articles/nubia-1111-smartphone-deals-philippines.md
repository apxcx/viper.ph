nubia Philippines is taking its NEOverse TAKEOVER campaign into the 11.11 shopping season with smartphone discounts, purchase perks and community events. Manila Standard reported that selected devices will be offered at up to 50% off on Shopee, while some nubia Music 2 units will appear in PHP11 promotions. The campaign is aimed at shoppers comparing gaming, entertainment and everyday phones during one of the Philippines’ busiest online sales periods. Availability, stock and platform mechanics will determine which advertised offers customers can actually claim.

![nubia 11.11 campaign product lineup](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/nubia-1111-smartphone-deals-philippines/image-1.webp?v=202611090910)

The headline deals cover the nubia Neo 5 Max and nubia Neo 5 GT 5G, while eligible purchases can include freebies worth up to PHP4,000. The package may also include a three-month Viu Philippines Premium subscription and nubia LiveBuds. These extras make the campaign more than a simple price cut, but buyers should still check the final product page, voucher rules and delivery terms before paying. Promotional pricing can vary by platform, and the lowest advertised price may depend on limited quantities or a specific checkout condition.

![nubia 11.11 NEOverse TAKEOVER team poster](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/nubia-1111-smartphone-deals-philippines/image-2.webp?v=202611090910)

nubia is also taking the campaign beyond online storefronts through the NEOverse SHOWDOWN 2026 in Dasmariñas, Cavite. The community event will feature a nubia Pro League Mobile Legends: Bang Bang tournament, DanceSiklaban, outreach activities and fiesta-inspired programming. Organizers said prizes will exceed PHP111,000. That local component connects a device promotion with the communities that use gaming phones for competition and entertainment. It also gives shoppers a physical way to experience the brand while the 11.11 sale remains primarily an e-commerce event.

![nubia 11.11 product and voucher promotion](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/nubia-1111-smartphone-deals-philippines/image-3.webp?v=202611090910)

The campaign continues after the main sale with nubia Brand Day, NEOverse TAKEOVER: The After Party, scheduled for November 13–15. Online product showcases, live entertainment, deal reveals and TikTok sessions with ONIC Philippines are planned. For Filipino buyers, the campaign creates several decision points: whether to prioritize gaming performance, audio features, price or bundled value. The useful comparison is not simply the largest discount. It is the final cost, warranty, software support and suitability of the device for the work and entertainment the buyer actually needs.
