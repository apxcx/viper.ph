Smart has become the first Philippine telecommunications company to integrate the Philippine Statistics Authority’s National ID Authentication Services into SIM registration. PSA says the National ID eVerify connection can reduce the process from roughly three minutes to less than one minute. The initial rollout covers Smart Prepaid and TNT subscribers who submit their name and complete live selfie verification. The change targets a familiar friction point in mobile onboarding while giving the telco a stronger way to confirm identity before a number is activated under the country’s SIM Registration Act.

![Mini, micro, and nano SIM card formats](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/smart-national-id-speeds-sim-registration/image-1.webp?v=202611280705)

The integration uses PSA’s identity-authentication service rather than asking subscribers to rely only on manual document checks. In the registration flow, a user’s information is matched through the National ID system while the live selfie step helps confirm that the person completing the transaction is present. PSA describes the arrangement as a way to make registration faster without dropping identity controls. For Smart and TNT users, the practical result is a shorter digital process that can be completed on a phone when the required details and connectivity are available.

![Globe Telecom SIM card package](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/smart-national-id-speeds-sim-registration/image-2.webp?v=202611280705)

The move also matters because SIM registration is tied to the government’s effort to reduce anonymous mobile accounts used in scams, fraud, and other abuse. Faster verification does not remove the need for accurate information or compliance with the rules. It gives telcos an electronic route to check a subscriber’s identity against a national credential. PSA says Smart is the first telco partner for the service, leaving room for other operators to adopt similar authentication as the infrastructure and implementation requirements become clearer.

![Close-up of a mobile phone SIM card](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/smart-national-id-speeds-sim-registration/image-3.webp?v=202611280705)

For consumers, the rollout points to a broader shift in how public digital identity can support everyday services. A national ID check that works inside a telco registration flow could eventually reduce repeated form-filling across other regulated transactions, although each use still depends on the agency and company involved. Smart’s current availability for Prepaid and TNT customers is therefore an early implementation, not a universal replacement for every registration method. Its success will depend on reliable verification, clear consent, privacy safeguards, and access for users who need alternatives.
