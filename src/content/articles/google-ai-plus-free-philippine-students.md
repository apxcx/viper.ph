Google is offering eligible college and university students in the Philippines**one free year of Google AI Plus**, with registration open until December 31, 2026. YugaTech reports that the plan includes Study Notebooks, which can use uploaded syllabi, slides, and reading files to help create study plans, practice tests, learning guides, and progress tracking.

The offer also includes access to Gemini Omni, Gemini Live, interactive 3D visualizations for selected subjects, and 400GB shared across Drive, Gmail, and Photos. Students must verify active enrollment through Google’s student hub and provide a payment method, although Google says there will be no charges during the free period. Users should review renewal terms and cancel before billing if they do not want to continue.
