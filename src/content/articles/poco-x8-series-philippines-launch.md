POCO has launched the X8, X8s and C95 Pro in the Philippines, giving buyers three new choices across the midrange and entry-level smartphone segments. BusinessWorld reports that the X8s starts at PHP24,999, while the X8 starts at PHP23,999 and the C95 Pro starts at PHP13,999. The lineup is timed with a First Day Sale running from Nov. 6 to 15, when the X8 and X8s receive a PHP2,000 discount. That window makes the launch immediately relevant to shoppers comparing holiday-season upgrades and value-focused devices.

![POCO X8 Pro lineup with Philippine pricing](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/poco-x8-series-philippines-launch/image-1.webp?v=202611060850)

The X8s is the performance-oriented option, pairing a 6.35-inch 1.5K AMOLED panel with a 1-120Hz adaptive refresh rate and peak brightness of up to 3,500 nits in supported scenarios. Its cameras include a 50-megapixel main sensor with optical image stabilization, a 50MP telephoto camera and an 8MP ultrawide. Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 processing, a 6,330mAh battery and 100-watt HyperCharge complete a specification sheet aimed at users who want gaming, photography and fast charging without moving into flagship pricing.

![POCO X8 Pro Iron Man Edition promotional display](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/poco-x8-series-philippines-launch/image-2.webp?v=202611060850)

The regular X8 takes a different value route. It uses a larger 6.83-inch 1.5K AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate and a silicon-carbon battery rated at 8,340mAh. Its Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 platform, 67W HyperCharge and dual rear-camera system keep the focus on endurance and everyday use. The C95 Pro sits lower in the range with a 7,500mAh battery, 45W turbo charging, a 6.9-inch display and a MediaTek Helio G91-Ultra chip. Those differences make the three phones easier to position by budget and priority.

![POCO X8 Pro Iron Man Edition with promotional pricing](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/poco-x8-series-philippines-launch/image-3.webp?v=202611060850)

Philippine buyers should still look beyond the headline specifications before purchasing. Battery size and charging speed matter, but long-term value also depends on software support, service availability, accessory pricing and how comfortably each phone fits a user’s network, camera and gaming needs. The X8s is the clearest choice for performance features, while the X8 emphasizes screen size and endurance. The C95 Pro offers the largest battery at a lower entry price. The First Day Sale may improve the calculation, but buyers should compare the final price with competing midrange models.
