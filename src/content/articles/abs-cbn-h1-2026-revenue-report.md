ABS-CBN Corporation posted a consolidated revenue of**P6.88 billion**in the first half of 2026, signaling steady recovery amid regulatory headwinds. The media giant continues to register growth in its**digital**and**cable**segments despite operating without a broadcast franchise, bolstering diversified income streams. Management credits expanding online platforms, streaming partnerships and targeted advertising solutions for offsetting terrestrial limitations. Analysts say sustained audience engagement across OTT and cable channels underpins the company’s ability to monetize content while preparing for broader distribution initiatives.

To accelerate its recovery, ABS-CBN is prioritizing**content syndication**and**international distribution**as core revenue drivers, deploying content deals and licensing arrangements across Asia and key diaspora markets. The firm is scaling partnerships with global streaming services and local cable operators to widen reach and secure recurring income. Executives emphasize investment in original programming and format sales to maximize intellectual property value, while using data-driven marketing to improve monetization and support margin recovery through the rest of 2026.
