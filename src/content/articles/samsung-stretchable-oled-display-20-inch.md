Samsung Display has unveiled new**Samsung stretchable OLED**technologies at IMID 2026 in Busan, including a 20-inch panel built through tiling technology. YugaTech reports that the display is being demonstrated for possible applications in automotive cockpits, humanoid robots, and digital signage. The company also presented Wide View OLED technology designed to maintain more uniform brightness across curved areas, addressing one of the challenges associated with foldable, slidable, and rollable screens.

The showcase extends beyond flexible panels. Samsung Display is also presenting OLED products for artificial intelligence, automotive, and gaming applications, including a 4K 360Hz QD-OLED monitor and a 300Hz OLED laptop display. The developments are demonstrations rather than announcements of immediate Philippine retail products, but they offer a look at the display formats that could reach future consumer devices and public installations.
