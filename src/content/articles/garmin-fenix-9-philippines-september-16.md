Garmin Philippines will release the**Fenix 9 and Fenix 9 Pro**multisport GPS smartwatches locally on September 16, 2026. YugaTech reports that prices start at PHP 62,690 for the Fenix 9 and PHP 72,690 for the Fenix 9 Pro. Both models focus on AMOLED displays, upgraded maps, and training features, while the Pro variant adds a brighter panel, a full-titanium case, and the option for phone-free connectivity through Garmin’s inReach satellite and LTE service.

The Fenix 9 Pro reaches around 3,000 nits of peak brightness and offers up to 64GB of storage for maps, music, and workouts. Its 43mm, 47mm, and 51mm sizes can support two-way messaging, live tracking, and SOS with an active inReach plan. Philippine availability will include Garmin.com, Garmin Brand Stores, specialty stores, official e-commerce channels, and Kinetic pre-orders. Source:[YugaTech](https://www.yugatech.com/news/garmin-fenix-9-fenix-9-pro-specs-and-price-in-ph/).
