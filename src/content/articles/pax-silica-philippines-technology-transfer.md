Critics are urging the Philippine government to make**technology transfer**a central requirement in deals tied to the US-led Pax Silica initiative. ABS-CBN News reports that technology advocates want agreements to do more than attract foreign investment: they should also help Filipinos build expertise, companies, and ownership in advanced electronics and semiconductor manufacturing. The discussion comes as the Philippines weighs how to gain lasting economic value from its strategic role in the regional technology supply chain.

Proposals include time-bound targets for Filipino capability in circuit-board production, testing, and advanced packaging, as well as stronger participation by local firms and joint ventures. BCDA President and CEO Joshua Bingcang said officials are reviewing concerns and that a framework could be signed in November. The debate asks how foreign capital can create**durable local technology capacity**rather than dependence.
