XPPen has introduced the**Magic Pro 13**drawing tablet at IFA 2026, targeting creators who need a portable device that can also serve as a PC drawing display. YugaTech reports that its 12.95-inch 3K panel runs at 120Hz, covers 100% of sRGB and 96% of DCI-P3, and supports the company’s Drawing Display Mode through a single cable.

The tablet uses a MediaTek Dimensity 8300 processor, 8GB of RAM, 256GB of storage expandable to 2TB, and a 10,200mAh battery. Its X4 stylus supports 16,384 pressure levels, tilt recognition, and under-30ms response. Pricing was not announced, but sales are expected in select markets in September. Source:[YugaTech](https://www.yugatech.com/news/ifa-2026-xppen-magic-pro-13-goes-official/).
