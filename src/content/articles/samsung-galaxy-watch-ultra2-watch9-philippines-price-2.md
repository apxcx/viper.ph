Samsung has officially expanded its wearable ecosystem in the Philippines with the introduction of the**Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra2**and the Galaxy Watch9. Designed for those who push their limits, the**Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra2**features a rugged titanium build and advanced GPS capabilities, making it the perfect companion for outdoor adventures and extreme sports. The new series also introduces enhanced BioActive sensors, providing more accurate health tracking and personalized wellness insights for Filipino users looking to maintain an active lifestyle.

Alongside the Ultra model, the Galaxy Watch9 offers a more refined and classic design suitable for everyday wear. Both the**Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra2**and Watch9 come integrated with the latest Wear OS, offering seamless connectivity with other Galaxy devices and access to a wide range of apps. Local pricing and pre-order bundles have also been announced, with exclusive freebies available for early adopters. With these new wearables, Samsung continues to lead the way in health and fitness technology, empowering Pinoys to take better control of their well-being.
