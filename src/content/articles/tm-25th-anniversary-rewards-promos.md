**TM is marking its 25th anniversary with a year-long 2GEDER 5EVER campaign**that combines subscriber rewards, support services, digital offers and community activities. The Globe brand’s prepaid unit unveiled the program at the Globe Q3 Blueprint, positioning the anniversary around everyday value for its customer base rather than a single limited-time sale. The campaign will use digital, retail, SMS, Facebook and on-ground channels throughout the anniversary year.

According to Manila Standard, TM Rewards Points as Cash is expanding to cover practical expenses such as**groceries and fuel**. TM is also offering Emergency Loans through SMS and Facebook for a ₱1 service fee. Customers should check the official terms and eligibility requirements before registering because availability, qualification, repayment conditions and offer mechanics may differ by service and account.

The anniversary program also includes a**TM 25th Freebie**attached to every eligible promo registration starting in September. The reported benefit is one day of unlimited access to TikTok and Facebook, while partner flash deals are planned through the GlobeOne app every 25th of the month. The exact promo registration mechanics and any limits should be confirmed through TM’s official customer channels before consumers rely on an offer.

TM’s community activities include nationwide TM Caravans and a**TM TeaM Fair Concert on September 12**, with SB19, Jayat, Janine Berdin and local bands listed in the report. For Philippine subscribers, the campaign is notable because it links mobile connectivity with rewards, emergency support and live events. TM customers can visit the official TM Tambayan or Globe pages for current eligibility, dates and terms.
