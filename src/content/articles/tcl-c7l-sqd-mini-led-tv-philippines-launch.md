TCL Philippines has officially expanded its premium home entertainment lineup with the launch of the**TCL C7L SQD**Mini LED TV. Unveiled during a high-energy event at Nokal in Makati, the new flagship television promises to redefine the viewing experience for Filipino homes. The**TCL C7L SQD**features advanced Mini LED technology, delivering exceptional peak brightness, deep blacks, and a wide color gamut that rivals professional-grade monitors, making it an ideal choice for both cinematic viewing and high-end gaming.

Beyond its impressive display, the**TCL C7L SQD**comes integrated with the latest Google TV interface, providing seamless access to popular streaming services and smart home controls. The launch event also showcased TCL’s commitment to the Philippine market, highlighting localized service support and competitive warranty packages. With the**TCL C7L SQD**, the brand aims to solidify its position as a leader in the large-screen segment, offering cutting-edge technology at a price point that remains accessible to the growing middle class.
