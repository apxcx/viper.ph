**Smart Communications has introduced the Home WiFi SIM 99**, a prepaid SIM designed for compatible home WiFi routers. Smart’s official online store lists the SIM at PHP99 with 2GB of data that does not expire. The offer is intended for open-line home WiFi devices and for older Smart or PLDT Home WiFi routers whose previous SIM has expired or become inactive. The product is not intended for smartphones, tablets, or pocket WiFi devices, so compatibility should be checked before purchase.

**The SIM supports Smart’s 4G and 5G network and can be reloaded with home WiFi promos.**Smart’s product listing says customers can use it with Home WiFi BIG DATA or UNLI DATA offers after the included data is used. The SIM-only format is contract-free, which may appeal to households that already own a compatible router and want to avoid replacing the device. It is also a different product from a mobile broadband SIM, because the official listing limits its allowed devices to WiFi routers.

**Smart lists nationwide delivery options for the product**, with lead times shown for Metro Manila, Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao. The official store page also lists free shipping nationwide and payment options including cards, Maya, GCash, and QR Ph. However, the same official page currently displays the item as out of stock. Availability can change, so customers should confirm the live listing or check Smart Stores, authorized retailers, and distributors before relying on the product for a specific connectivity need.

**The Home WiFi SIM 99 gives households a low-cost way to reactivate compatible equipment**, but its value depends on router support, current stock, signal quality, and the user’s data needs. YugaTech reported that the SIM is available through Smart Stores, authorized retailers, distributors, and the Smart Online Store, while Smart’s own listing confirms the PHP99 price and 2GB no-expiry inclusion. Because availability and retail terms may change, customers should verify the current product page and applicable Smart promo rules before buying.
