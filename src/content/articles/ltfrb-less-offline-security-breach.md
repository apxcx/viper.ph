The Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board has taken its**Electronic Support System (LESS)**offline after confirming a security breach, putting every LESS-linked service and transaction on hold until further notice. ABS-CBN News reported the move on September 21, citing the agency’s statement. LTFRB also told concerned offices and personnel not to access or process transactions through the platform until an official restoration notice. The shutdown is a precaution while investigators secure the system and establish what happened for now.

The investigation brings together the**Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT)**, the Cybercrime Investigation and Coordinating Center (CICC), and the National Privacy Commission (NPC). LTFRB’s technical teams are validating information and assessing the incident’s impact, while the NPC is checking which data, if any, were affected. Philippine Daily Inquirer and GMA News independently reported the outage and agency coordination. The work remains active, so the confirmed record is a breach investigation—not a completed public accounting of exposed files or individuals.

LESS is the LTFRB’s digital platform and internal data environment for online applications, transactions, and electronic support for public utility vehicle operators and motorists. That makes the temporary suspension a direct**public-service disruption in the Philippines**, even though road operations themselves have not been reported as stopped. The NPC said it received LTFRB’s breach notification on September 13, according to ABS-CBN and The Philippine Star. LTFRB has not announced a restoration date, and offices have been instructed to wait for verified guidance.

For now, readers should treat the confirmed scope narrowly: LESS is offline, related services are suspended, and DICT, CICC, and NPC are investigating. LTFRB has not publicly identified the affected records, confirmed data exfiltration, named an attacker, or published a complete impact assessment.**No international impact is established**; this report concerns a Philippine government platform and does not extend the incident to overseas systems, tests, rumors, or allegations. Watch LTFRB and NPC announcements for verified updates before changing account or transaction decisions.
