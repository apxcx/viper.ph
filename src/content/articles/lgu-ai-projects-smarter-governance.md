Four Philippine local government units are preparing to showcase artificial intelligence applications built through PLDT and Smart Communications’ AI-in-a-Box program. Newsbytes reported that Baguio City, Cabanatuan City, Cebu City and Lanao del Sur developed projects aimed at public services and government operations. Their work will be discussed at the AI in Action Forum, where the central question is how promising prototypes can become dependable systems. For local governments, that shift matters because a demonstration is easier than maintaining software, data and accountability after a pilot ends.

![PLDT-Smart AI in Action forum panel discussion](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/lgu-ai-projects-smarter-governance/image-1.webp?v=202611090900)

The projects can support routine processing, access to government services, administrative work and data analysis for local officials. The forum will focus on readiness, infrastructure, governance frameworks and the skills employees need to operate AI tools. That puts implementation ahead of novelty. An application that speeds permits or helps answer public questions still needs reliable records, clear escalation paths and staff who can recognize an incorrect result. The participating LGUs offer different operating environments, so their experiences may show which ideas travel well and which require local design.

![PLDT-Smart AI in Action award recipients](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/lgu-ai-projects-smarter-governance/image-2.webp?v=202611090900)

Privacy and cybersecurity will be part of the conversation, alongside digital sovereignty. DILG and DOST-ASTI are expected to provide views on government AI adoption and the Philippines’ National AI Strategy, while ePLDT and IBM Philippines will discuss infrastructure and implementation. ARTA and the Development Academy of the Philippines will connect the technology discussion to government efficiency and national development. That mix is important because public-sector AI is not only a procurement decision. It also involves legal duties, institutional trust and the ability to explain automated recommendations.

![PLDT-Smart AI in Action forum speakers](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/lgu-ai-projects-smarter-governance/image-3.webp?v=202611090900)

For Philippine cities and provinces considering similar tools, the practical test will be sustainability. LGUs will need budgets for maintenance, secure data handling, model updates and training after technology partners leave the room. They also need metrics that show whether residents receive faster, clearer or more accessible services. The PLDT-Smart forum positions AI-in-a-Box as a starting point, not a finished digital-transformation strategy. The next phase will reveal whether local pilots can become shared public infrastructure without sacrificing human review or community accountability.
