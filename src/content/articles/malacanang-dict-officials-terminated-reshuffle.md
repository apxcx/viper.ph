Malacañang on August 15, 2026 confirmed the termination of two senior officials at the Department of Information and Communications Technology in a sweeping organizational reshuffle. The administration said the move is intended to streamline operations and enhance delivery of digital services. Officials described the changes as part of a broader push to accelerate the agency’s digital transformation and improve performance metrics. Stakeholders were told the reshuffle targets management bottlenecks and aligns leadership with new efficiency goals.**DICT Senior Officials Terminated**signals a renewed emphasis on measurable outcomes across the agency.

The Palace said the dismissals follow a review of agency performance and will be accompanied by a realignment of senior roles to hasten project delivery. DICT leadership will undergo a selection process for successors, with interim appointments expected while the agency refocuses on cybersecurity, broadband expansion and e-government services. Observers said the reshuffle could speed procurement and reduce duplication across units, improving timelines for flagship initiatives.**Officials will be monitored against clear KPIs**, and Malacañang emphasized accountability as central to achieving the government’s digital transformation objectives.
