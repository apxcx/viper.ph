UST and eCloudvalley Technology Philippines have announced a strategic reseller and co-delivery partnership aimed at accelerating**enterprise AI Philippines**adoption. The Manila Times reports that the alliance combines UST’s AI solutions and implementation expertise with eCloudvalley’s AWS capabilities, enterprise relationships, and local delivery network. The companies will target industries including retail, quick-service restaurants, consumer goods, travel and hospitality, and fashion, helping organizations deploy AI without building every capability in-house.

The portfolio includes Voice AI Pro, Smart Shelves, Virtual Try-On, Recommendation Engine, ContentPro, and Vision Checkout, alongside UST’s generative and agentic AI capabilities. Under the agreement, eCloudvalley will resell and co-deliver the solutions on AWS infrastructure, giving Philippine businesses access to globally developed tools with local support. The partnership reflects a shift from AI experimentation toward measurable business use cases, particularly for organizations seeking faster deployment, scalable cloud capacity, and accountability from an in-country implementation partner.
