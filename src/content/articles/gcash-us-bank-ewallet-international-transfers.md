GCash’s International Transfers feature now supports sending money from GCash wallets to supported U.S. financial institutions, according to a September 30 BusinessWorld report. The company says the route is intended for individuals, families, micro, small and medium enterprises, and other business users. GCash’s help center independently lists the United States as a supported bank destination and shows the in-app flow.**The update concerns a supported U.S. corridor, not proof that every bank, e-wallet or account is eligible.**Recipient availability and final terms remain visible in the app.

Users start in the GCash app by selecting Transfer, then International, choosing the United States, selecting a payout channel and institution, and entering recipient details. The help center lists a ₱500 minimum transfer, while maximums depend on wallet and transaction limits. Fees vary by amount and country and appear on the confirmation page.**There is no single public fee or exchange-rate promise that applies to every transfer.**Readers should review the app’s final quote before confirming a payment, especially for business suppliers or recurring family support.

BusinessWorld describes the service as near real time, but GCash’s own U.S. processing table adds an important qualification: bank transfers can take three business days when sent by the listed U.S. Eastern-time cutoff. Transactions after the cutoff, on weekends or holidays may process on the next business day.**Near real time should therefore be attributed, not rewritten as an instant-delivery guarantee.**The sources do not establish a universal success rate, complete list of supported U.S. institutions, or fixed delivery time for every payout channel.

The feature gives Philippine users a direct in-app route for selected U.S. payments without leaving the GCash workflow. Mynt previously said International Transfers covered 16 countries, including the United States, so this report is best framed as a fresh U.S.-corridor capability update rather than a first-ever international-transfer launch.**Convenience is clearer than speed: users still need to check eligibility, limits, fees, exchange rates and processing estimates.**G-Xchange’s BSP-regulated status, stated in the help center, does not certify a particular price or delivery promise.
