Samsung Electronics has announced an official partnership with**Activision Blizzard**for the upcoming**Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4**, naming**Samsung**the official TV, monitor and soundbar partner. The collaboration will focus on technical optimizations for**HDR10+ GAMING**, delivering enhanced contrast and color fidelity on Samsung displays.**Samsung**also plans to surface exclusive game access through the**Samsung Gaming Hub**, promising streamlined discovery and optimized profiles for its ecosystem. The deal underlines Samsung’s drive to position its hardware as a premium platform for flagship AAA releases.

**Samsung**will also livestream the official**Call of Duty: Next**event on August 21 via the**Samsung Gaming Hub**, giving users direct access to trailers, developer commentary and special promotions ahead of launch. The partnership promises curated display presets and audio tuning tailored to**Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4**, leveraging Samsung’s soundbars and monitors for competitive and cinematic play. For gamers and buyers, the move signals tighter integration between hardware and software, with Samsung positioned to offer exclusive content, early access windows and an optimized viewing experience on its certified devices.
