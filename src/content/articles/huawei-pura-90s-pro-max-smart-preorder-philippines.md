Smart Communications has officially opened**pre-orders**for the**HUAWEI Pura 90s Pro**and**Pura 90s Pro Max**in the Philippines, marking the local debut of Huawei’s flagship photography lineup. Available through Smart’s retail and online channels, the new models feature a standout**200MP Periscope Telephoto Camera**and native**5G**connectivity for faster uploads and low-latency streaming. Early buyers via Smart can secure limited-time bundles and priority delivery. The move positions Smart as a key partner in bringing advanced mobile imaging and network capabilities to Filipino consumers.

Pre-order customers who reserve the**HUAWEI Pura 90s Pro**or**Pura 90s Pro Max**through**Smart Communications**can claim exclusive bundles, including the**HUAWEI Watch GT 4**and**FreeBuds Pro 3**, plus select plan credits. Smart’s offers aim to enhance the flagship experience with wearable and audio accessories tuned to the phones’ capabilities. Pricing, color options and exact bundle inventory vary by store; interested buyers should visit Smart’s website or authorized outlets to compare promos and secure theirs during the pre-order window before stocks run out.
