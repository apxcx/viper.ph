The Philippines has launched a**Semiconductor and Electronics Industry Roadmap**intended to attract more foreign investment and move the country toward higher-value chip design, engineering, and manufacturing. BusinessMirror reports that the newly formed Semiconductor and Electronics Industry Advisory Council will help investors navigate registration, permits, and facility setup instead of leaving companies to manage fragmented government processes on their own.

The roadmap sets 2030 targets that include USD110 billion in semiconductor and electronics exports, a larger share of global assembly, testing, and packaging, and a domestic integrated-circuit design sector. Its success will depend on execution, workforce training, reliable infrastructure, and whether investors see faster approvals in practice.
