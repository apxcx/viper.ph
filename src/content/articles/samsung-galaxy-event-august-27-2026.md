Samsung is set to hold its**Galaxy Event August 2026**on August 27, introducing a new addition to the Galaxy S26 family. In its official Philippine newsroom invitation, Samsung says the product is designed to bring core Galaxy experiences—from camera features to artificial intelligence and the latest One UI to users looking for practical everyday upgrades. The event is expected to reveal the device’s full specifications, pricing, and market details.

Filipino viewers can watch the livestream on Samsung.com and Samsung’s YouTube channel beginning at**8:00 PM Philippine time**, equivalent to 9:00 PM in Korea. Samsung has not disclosed every detail ahead of the presentation, so the announcement should be treated as a launch preview rather than confirmation of final local availability. Source:[Samsung Newsroom Philippines](https://news.samsung.com/ph/invitation-galaxy-event-august-2026).
