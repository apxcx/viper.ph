ASUS Philippines is offering buyers of eligible**ASUS and ROG laptops**three months of either Google AI Pro or Google AI Plus at no extra cost. YugaTech reports that the offer covers supported models across ROG, TUF Gaming, ProArt, Zenbook, and Vivobook families, including selected 2025, 2026, and upcoming 2027 devices in supported markets.

Google AI Pro includes higher access limits, advanced Gemini features, and 5TB of cloud storage, while Google AI Plus includes productivity tools and 400GB of storage. Depending on the plan, users may also access Deep Research, Gemini Notebook, Flow, and other services. Redemption is done through the MyASUS app, and buyers should check the full model list and terms before claiming.
