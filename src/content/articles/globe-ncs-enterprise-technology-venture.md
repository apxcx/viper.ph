Globe Telecom is moving further into enterprise technology through NCS Philippines, a joint venture with NCS, a Singtel Group company. The arrangement gives Globe a platform beyond traditional connectivity, targeting cloud computing, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and digital transformation. NCS Philippines combines NCS’s regional technology capabilities with more than two decades of local experience from Yondu, Globe’s technology subsidiary. The move reflects how Philippine telecom operators are seeking higher-value business services as companies and government agencies modernize their systems in the country.

![Globe Expands Enterprise Tech With NCS supporting image 1](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/globe-ncs-enterprise-technology-venture/image-1.webp?v=202611260800)

The venture has two main portfolios. Core IT Services covers enterprise applications, systems integration, cloud migration, infrastructure management, and cybersecurity. NEXT Digital Solutions focuses on newer deployments such as AI, predictive analytics, robotic process automation, smart-city applications, 5G enterprise networks, and edge computing. Together, the portfolios address both the maintenance of existing systems and the demand for new digital tools. That combination is important for industries such as banking, healthcare, and government, where reliability and data protection are central requirements.

![Globe Expands Enterprise Tech With NCS supporting image 2](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/globe-ncs-enterprise-technology-venture/image-2.webp?v=202611260800)

NCS holds a 51 percent majority stake while Globe retains 49 percent. The venture has an indicated enterprise baseline valuation of about P1.87 billion, and NCS brings access to approximately 14,000 technology professionals across 56 disciplines. Globe contributes market relationships and Yondu’s Philippine operations. The structure lets Globe participate in enterprise technology growth without building an entire regional IT-services business alone. It also gives NCS a stronger local route into organizations that need implementation, managed services, and technology support close to their operations.

![Globe Expands Enterprise Tech With NCS supporting image 3](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/globe-ncs-enterprise-technology-venture/image-3.webp?v=202611260800)

For customers, the practical question will be execution rather than the ownership structure. Philippine companies need partners that can connect strategy to secure migration, measurable productivity gains, and dependable operations. The venture’s exposure to AI and automation may create opportunities, but it also raises the need for skills, governance, and responsible deployment. Globe’s telecom business remains the financial base supporting its investments. NCS Philippines adds a separate growth path tied to enterprise technology demand, with results likely to depend on its ability to win and deliver complex projects.
