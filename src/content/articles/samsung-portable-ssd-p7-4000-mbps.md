Samsung has introduced the**Portable SSD P7**, an external solid-state drive designed for users who move large files between computers, cameras, and mobile workstations. YugaTech reports that the drive can reach transfer speeds of up to 4,000 MB/s, putting it in the conversation for video editors, photographers, developers, and other users who regularly handle large project folders.

The P7’s headline performance depends on compatible USB4 hardware, cables, and host systems, so actual speeds may vary across laptops and desktops. Local pricing and availability should be confirmed through Samsung Philippines or authorized retailers. For Filipino buyers, the product’s appeal will depend on capacity, sustained performance, heat management, warranty, and device compatibility.
