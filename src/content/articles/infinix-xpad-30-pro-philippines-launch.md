Infinix has launched the**XPAD 30 Pro**in the Philippines with a suggested retail price of PHP13,999 and a limited launch price of PHP12,999 through selected online stores. YugaTech reports that the tablet is aimed at users who want entertainment, light gaming, and mobile productivity in one portable device.

The tablet features an 11-inch 2.5K display, a MediaTek Helio G200 Ultimate processor, 128GB storage, up to 2TB microSD expansion, and 4G SIM connectivity. It also packs an 8,200mAh battery with 18W charging, XOS 16, and AI tools for notes, translation, and document scanning. Buyers may receive an XPENCIL, case, and one-year Viu Premium subscription under launch offers. Source:[YugaTech](https://www.yugatech.com/news/infinix-xpad-30-pro-launches-in-ph-for-php-13999/).
