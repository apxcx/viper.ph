TechLife has launched the**TechLife Pad Mini**in the Philippines, bringing a compact Android tablet with 4G LTE connectivity to local users. YugaTech reports that the device has an 8-inch LCD screen, a MediaTek Helio G80 chipset, and up to 12GB of dynamic RAM. The tablet is positioned for consumers who want a larger screen for streaming, schoolwork, and casual productivity without moving to a full-size notebook. Its local arrival adds another affordable option to the growing Philippine tablet market.

The tablet carries a suggested retail price of PHP 9,999, although launch promotions offer lower prices through selected online channels. YugaTech lists a PHP 7,699 TikTok Shop price, PHP 8,399 on Shopee, and PHP 8,999 on Lazada, with a free Pad Handle Case worth PHP 999 while supplies last. Availability and promotional pricing may change, so buyers should confirm the final price and seller details before checkout.
