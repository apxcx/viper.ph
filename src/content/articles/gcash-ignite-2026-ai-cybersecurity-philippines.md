GCash brought technology leaders, policymakers, and industry stakeholders together at the fifth**GCash iGnite 2026**Innovation Summit, where discussions centered on artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and digital trust. The Mynt Newsroom said the event explored how organizations can use emerging technologies responsibly while making digital financial services safer and more inclusive. The summit also highlighted the global reach of Philippine-grown innovation and the role of public-private cooperation in moving the country’s digital economy forward.

The program included tracks on AI-driven business, intelligent trust, agentic enterprises, and regulatory technology. It also featured GSafe: The Vanguards of Trust, described as GCash’s first organized cybersecurity and anti-fraud summit. The discussions are relevant to Filipino consumers and businesses as more financial activity moves online, requiring stronger safeguards against fraud and better awareness of AI-enabled attacks.
