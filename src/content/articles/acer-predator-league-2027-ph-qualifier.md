Acer has opened the Philippine qualifier for the**Asia Pacific Predator League 2027**, offering a total prize pool of ₱1 million across VALORANT and League of Legends. Each title carries ₱500,000 in prizes. Registration runs until October 15, with open qualifiers set for October 17 for VALORANT and October 18 for League of Legends, according to Inquirer Technology. The competition gives Philippine teams a route from local online matches to a regional final in Vietnam next January.

The schedule continues with group stages on October 24 and 25 for VALORANT, then October 30 and 31 for League of Legends. Online playoffs are planned for November 7 and 8, while LAN finals for both games are scheduled for late November. The format creates several checkpoints for teams to prove consistency, not just a single elimination day. It also gives organizers time to identify the Philippine representatives who can compete against other Asia Pacific qualifiers from the wider Asia Pacific region.

The ₱1-million pool will be divided among the top eight finishers in each game throughout the Philippine qualifier. Champions receive ₱200,000 and runners-up ₱120,000, while third- and fourth-place teams get ₱50,000 each. Teams finishing fifth through eighth receive ₱20,000 each. Beyond the cash and prestige, the two champions earn the right to represent the Philippines at the Asia Pacific Predator League 2027 Grand Finals in Vietnam, scheduled for January 2027, with international exposure and team growth.

For Philippine esports, the qualifier matters as both a tournament and a talent pipeline. Regular competition can help teams build coaching, broadcast and event experience, broadcast exposure and team discipline while giving players a clearer path toward international play. Interested teams must use the official registration links for VALORANT or League of Legends and check the tournament rules before entering. The prize money is significant, but the wider opportunity for players is the chance to represent the country on a regional stage.
