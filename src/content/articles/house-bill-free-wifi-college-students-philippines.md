On August 13, 2026, the House of Representatives approved a landmark bill granting college students nationwide free access to digital learning resources and campus Wi-Fi. The measure, championed by**Speaker Faustino “Bojie” Dy III**, mandates free on-campus broadband and government-supported online materials to tackle education inequality across the Philippines. Lawmakers said the policy aims to remove access barriers for low-income students, improve remote learning readiness, and standardize digital infrastructure at state and participating private universities. The vote moves the measure to the Senate for final approval and implementation planning.

The bill signals a major shift for Philippine higher education and the telecom industry, with anticipated partnerships between universities, internet service providers and edtech firms to expand campus networks and curate free content. For students, the policy could lower dropout risks and boost employability by widening access to online courses and research tools. Businesses offering networking equipment, digital platforms and cybersecurity services may see increased demand, while universities will need funding and technical support for sustained operations. Implementation details, funding sources and a rollout timeline will shape the measure’s real-world impact on consumers and the economy.
