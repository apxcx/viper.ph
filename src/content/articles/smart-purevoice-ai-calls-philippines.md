Smart Communications is bringing AI-powered noise cancellation to mobile calls through PureVoice, a service first made available to Smart Infinity members in the Philippines. Coverage from Inquirer Technology, The Philippine Star and BusinessMirror says the feature is designed to reduce distractions from traffic, crowds and music without requiring special headphones or a separate application. The launch places software-based voice enhancement inside the telecom service itself, making clearer calls less dependent on the hardware inside a customer’s phone. It also gives Smart a way to improve voice quality on older devices that remain common in the market.

![Call-center booth representing clearer voice communications](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/smart-purevoice-ai-calls-philippines/image-1.webp?v=202611080746)

PureVoice is designed for ordinary conversations rather than a studio-style audio setup. It can be used on phones without built-in noise cancellation, and subscribers do not need to install an app or change device settings. Smart says members can press 8# during a call to turn the feature on or off. That simple control matters because users may need to switch between a noisy street, a quiet room and a conversation where natural background sound remains useful. The control is intentionally familiar because it works through the dialer rather than asking customers to learn another interface.

![Call-center desk supporting everyday customer conversations](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/smart-purevoice-ai-calls-philippines/image-2.webp?v=202611080746)

The service reflects how Philippine telecom providers are moving beyond network coverage and data speeds to improve the quality of specific experiences. Smart has previously rolled out Voice over LTE and Video over LTE, and the company linked PureVoice to its broader investments in network modernization and AI capabilities. The initial Smart Infinity launch also creates a controlled environment for testing adoption, call quality and customer feedback before the feature reaches more subscribers. That phased approach can help the operator measure network behavior, support needs and whether customers notice a meaningful improvement in real-world calls.

![Mobile phone base station supporting telecom connectivity](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/smart-purevoice-ai-calls-philippines/image-3.webp?v=202611080746)

PureVoice will not remove every limitation of mobile voice service. The result can still depend on signal strength, microphone quality, language patterns and the complexity of the background sound. Users should also understand whether the feature changes call charges or eligibility under their plan. Still, the launch is notable because it treats call clarity as a software problem that can be improved after a phone is purchased. For Filipino subscribers, that could make everyday calls more usable in busy homes, streets and workplaces.
