In a major move to bolster national cybersecurity, the**BPI Gogolook Partnership**has been officially launched to protect Filipino consumers from the rising threat of digital fraud. BPI, one of the country’s leading financial institutions, has teamed up with Gogolook, the developer of the popular caller ID and spam detection app Whoscall. This strategic collaboration aims to integrate advanced scam detection technologies into the banking experience, helping users identify and block suspicious calls and messages that could lead to phishing or unauthorized account access.

The**BPI Gogolook Partnership**comes at a critical time when digital scams are becoming increasingly sophisticated, targeting unsuspecting bank clients through various social engineering tactics. Representatives from both companies emphasized that the initiative is not just about technology, but also about educating the public on how to stay safe in the digital age. By leveraging Gogolook’s massive global database of suspicious numbers, BPI aims to provide an additional layer of security for its millions of users, ensuring that digital banking remains a safe and reliable option for all Filipinos.
