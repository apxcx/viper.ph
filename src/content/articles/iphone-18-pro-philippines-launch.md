Apple’s iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max are now available in the Philippines after Power Mac Center held simultaneous midnight launches in 15 locations nationwide. The events covered Greater Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao, including Greenbelt 3, SM Mall of Asia, SM City Iloilo, SM Lanang and SM City Clark. ABS-CBN separately reported Filipino customers picking up the new phones on launch day. The availability confirms a local retail release, while exact stock and store-level terms can still vary by location.

Power Mac Center said customers could purchase or collect pre-ordered devices at participating stores, with launch activities and limited freebies for people in line before the cutoff. The new Pro models are also available through Power Mac Center’s nationwide stores and official Web Store. Buyers can use 1 Infinite loyalty points and a range of bank offers, but discounts, minimum spends and installment periods differ by provider. Those conditions make it important to check the current retailer page and card issuer terms before treating a headline offer as universal.

The launch promotions include extra discounts, cashback and zero-interest installment options from banks such as BPI, Metrobank, BDO, UnionBank, RCBC, Security Bank, PNB, EastWest, HSBC and China Bank. Home Credit also lists a zero-downpayment, zero-interest option within its stated period. Some offers end on October 18, while others run into November or January 2027. GCash terms cited by Manila Bulletin include cashback with a minimum purchase and a limited October 9–16 window, so buyers should confirm expiry dates before checkout.

Power Mac Center also extended online purchasing through its Web Store and official Lazada Flagship Store. Until October 18, the retailer is offering bundle pricing when an iPhone 18 Pro or Pro Max is bought with selected Apple Watch, AirPods or accessory products. The practical takeaway is that Philippine availability is no longer the question; comparison now shifts to stock, warranty, payment method and total bundle cost.
