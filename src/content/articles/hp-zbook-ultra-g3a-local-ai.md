**HP has unveiled the ZBook Ultra G3a 16**, a mobile workstation designed to run demanding professional software and large AI workloads locally. Announced on September 15, the device is powered by up to an AMD Ryzen AI Max+ PRO 495 processor and can be configured with up to 192GB of unified memory. HP says up to 160GB can be allocated to graphics memory, helping the workstation handle bigger local models and simultaneous professional applications. The announcement is global, and HP says availability is expected in October 2026, with pricing to be announced later.

**The ZBook Ultra G3a is aimed at professionals who need local AI without moving every task to the cloud.**HP says the workstation can run models of up to 300 billion parameters locally, depending on configuration and workload. That capability is paired with an integrated AMD Radeon graphics architecture, a neural-processing unit rated at up to 55 TOPS, and storage of up to 8TB. Local inference can help reduce latency and give organizations more control over sensitive project data, although the practical result will depend on software support, model optimization, and the specific configuration customers choose.

**HP is also positioning the machine as a workflow tool rather than only a faster laptop.**Perplexity Computer will come preloaded, while HP cites support for professional applications including Autodesk Revit, Autodesk 3ds Max, and Chaos Enscape. The company says selected workflows can keep project context on the device and use local AI alongside connected tools. HP also lists optional 5G connectivity through HP Go, a replaceable keyboard, and a design that starts at 4.2 pounds and is 17.9 millimeters thin, giving mobile teams more flexibility away from a desk.

**The launch reflects a broader shift toward personal and on-device AI for engineering, design, and technical work.**AMD separately highlighted the ZBook Ultra G3a at Autodesk University 2026, linking its Ryzen AI Max PRO 400 Series processors to local models, Autodesk applications, and MCP-enabled workflows. The product is not yet a Philippine availability announcement: HP’s newsroom release says the workstation is expected in October 2026, while pricing will be shared closer to launch. Philippine buyers should therefore treat current details as global product information until local channels publish confirmed terms.
