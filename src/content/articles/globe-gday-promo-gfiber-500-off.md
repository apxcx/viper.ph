Globe is celebrating GDay with a broadband promotion for existing mobile postpaid customers. According to Globe’s official announcement, eligible GPlan subscribers can receive**up to ₱500 off their monthly mobile bill for two years**when they get an eligible GFiber plan. The offer links mobile and home connectivity in one package, but customers should review the official terms before switching because eligibility, plan availability, and other conditions apply.

The promotion is aimed specifically at existing GPlan customers rather than all Globe users. Globe’s post directs interested subscribers to its GFiber offer page and says the discount can reach ₱500 per month across the two-year period. The practical value will depend on the customer’s current mobile plan, the selected GFiber package, installation or other fees, and whether the offer can be combined with existing account benefits. Customers should confirm the final monthly bill before completing an upgrade.

Globe’s announcement also positions GFiber as part of a broader home-connectivity lineup, with plans advertised at different speeds and service conditions. A broadband promotion is most useful when the advertised speed, data policy, contract period, installation process, and service coverage are clear. Subscribers comparing offers should check whether the discount changes after the promotional period, whether a lock-in applies, and what happens if they cancel or downgrade before the two years are complete.

The offer shows how Philippine telcos are increasingly bundling mobile and fixed internet services to retain customers and raise household connectivity. For consumers, the important comparison is the total cost over the full commitment—not only the headline discount. Globe customers should use the official GFiber channel linked in the announcement, confirm that their account qualifies, and read the complete promotional terms, including any expiry date, trade permit information, service limitations, and billing requirements.
