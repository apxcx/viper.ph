Smart Communications has reduced the price of its**5G Max Home WiFi**to PHP2,995 from PHP4,990 in a promotion running until November 20, 2026. Gadget Pilipinas reports that the package includes the router, a Smart SIM, and 15 days of unlimited data. The offer targets households that need faster connectivity but may not have access to fiber installation in their area.

The plug-and-play 5G Fixed Wireless Access device supports speeds of up to 600Mbps and as many as 40 connected devices. After the free data period, customers can reload with UNLI 1299 for 30 days. Actual speeds will depend on coverage, network conditions, and location, so buyers should check Smart’s service availability before purchasing. Source:[Gadget Pilipinas](https://www.gadgetpilipinas.net/2026/09/smart-5g-max-home-wifi-40-off/).
