Xiaomi is set to launch the**Xiaomi 18 Fold**in China on September 7 at 7 PM, introducing what the company calls a middle-folding form factor. YugaTech reports that the design aims to balance one-handed use when closed with a larger viewing area when opened, positioning the device between a compact phone and a traditional book-style foldable.

The phone is expected to have a 5.38-inch external display and a 7.58-inch internal screen. Xiaomi has not yet confirmed Philippine availability, local pricing, or a complete specification sheet, so the launch event will be important for details on the hinge, cameras, chipset, software, and international plans. Source:[YugaTech](https://www.yugatech.com/news/xiaomi-18-fold-set-to-launch-on-september-7/).
