Acer has announced a broad product lineup at IFA 2026, including the**EP130K ePaper Monitor**, portable displays, a projector, Android tablets, and new 5G connectivity hardware. YugaTech reports that the 13-inch EP130K offers a paper-like display with up to 3,200 by 2,400 resolution, 60Hz refresh, and touch support, while the PD163Q P3 uses three foldable 15.6-inch Full HD panels.

The company also introduced the C350e portable projector, Iconia tablets, the Connect X6ES 5G router, and Connect D5P 5G dongle. Announced prices start at USD749.99 for the EP130K, but Philippine release dates and local pricing remain unconfirmed. Buyers should wait for Acer Philippines details before treating the products as locally available.
