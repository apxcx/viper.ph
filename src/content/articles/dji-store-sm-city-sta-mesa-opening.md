Drone enthusiasts and content creators in Manila have a new reason to celebrate as**DJI SM City Sta. Mesa**officially opens its doors today, August 19, 2026. The new authorised store features a complete lineup of DJI’s latest innovations, from the portable Mini series to the professional-grade Mavic drones. The opening event at**DJI SM City Sta. Mesa**drew a large crowd of tech enthusiasts eager to experience hands-on demos and exclusive launch-day discounts on popular accessories like the Osmo Pocket and Action series.

The strategic location at**DJI SM City Sta. Mesa**aims to cater to the growing community of aerial photographers and videographers in the central Manila area. Store representatives highlighted that the new branch will also serve as a hub for after-sales support and workshops, helping Pinoy pilots fly safely and creatively. Whether you are a hobbyist looking for your first drone or a professional filmmaker seeking the latest gimbal technology, the new**DJI SM City Sta. Mesa**store is now fully equipped to meet all your creative needs.
