The Land Bank of the Philippines is exploring cooperation with**NPCI International Payments Limited**, the international arm of the organization behind India’s Unified Payments Interface. LANDBANK said the discussions cover potential digital-payments interoperability and broader financial inclusion. The engagement took place during President Ferdinand Marcos Jr.’s participation in the BRICS Summit in India. It is an exploratory development: the official bank release and Philippine Information Agency report do not announce a signed agreement, live integration, launch date, fees, or a new service for Philippine consumers.

LANDBANK said the talks could examine**cross-border payments, government collections and disbursements, and access to digital financial services**for overseas Filipinos and underserved communities. Interoperable systems could eventually make transactions more seamless for businesses, agencies, and Filipinos abroad, but those are proposed benefits rather than evidence of an operating product for Filipino users. The bank’s language is careful, using terms such as “exploring” and “potential cooperation” instead of promising a rollout or confirming that India’s UPI is already available locally.

The Philippine bank also met Pine Labs to discuss possible cooperation involving**digital onboarding, merchant acquisition, QR-enabled transactions, and artificial-intelligence applications**. LANDBANK described the conversations as part of a wider effort to improve technology-driven financial inclusion. Separately, the bank held a financial-education forum for Filipinos in New Delhi, introducing participants to its digital-banking solutions, cybersecurity initiatives, and investment opportunities. These activities show a broad engagement with India’s fintech ecosystem, not a single consumer launch announcement across the wider financial system.

For Filipinos, the potential impact would depend on technical, regulatory, and commercial decisions that have not yet been disclosed. A future connection could matter for overseas workers, government payments, agencies handling cross-border transactions, and businesses serving foreign customers. For now, the confirmed story is that LANDBANK is studying possible partnerships with NIPL and Pine Labs. Readers should treat claims about a Philippine UPI rollout, QR Ph integration, pricing, or availability as**unconfirmed**until LANDBANK or the relevant regulators announce them.
