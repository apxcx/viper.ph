TP-Link has unveiled a complete**Wi-Fi 8 lineup**at IFA 2026, led by the Archer 8 Ultra router and Deco 8 Ultra mesh system. YugaTech reports that the new generation is aimed at households and businesses handling more simultaneous devices, high-resolution streaming, gaming, and connected-home traffic. The lineup signals a move toward more capable wireless networks as phones, laptops, televisions, cameras, and smart-home products continue to share the same connection.

The company’s launch portfolio includes both traditional router and mesh options, giving users different ways to improve coverage and capacity. Philippine pricing and release timing were not detailed in the initial report, so buyers should wait for local announcements before treating the products as available. Existing Wi-Fi 7 users may also want to compare real-world benefits, compatibility, and upgrade costs before switching.
