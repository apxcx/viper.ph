OPPO Philippines has officially announced the retail availability of the**OPPO Reno16 5G**and Reno16 Pro 5G across the country. Dubbed as the new ‘Portrait Expert,’ the series brings significant upgrades in mobile photography, featuring a new AI-enhanced sensor that captures professional-grade portraits even in challenging lighting conditions. The**OPPO Reno16 5G**series also debuts a more refined ‘Glow’ design, making it one of the most stylish mid-range contenders in the local market this year.

Aside from its imaging prowess, the**OPPO Reno16 5G**series is equipped with ultra-fast charging technology and a high-refresh-rate AMOLED display, catering to the needs of Filipino mobile gamers and content consumers. Local pricing starts at competitive points, with various installment plans available through major telcos and retail partners. With the launch of the**OPPO Reno16 5G**, OPPO continues to solidify its position as a top-of-mind brand for young, tech-savvy Filipinos looking for a balance of performance and aesthetics.
