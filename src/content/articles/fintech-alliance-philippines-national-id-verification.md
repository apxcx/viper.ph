**FinTech Alliance Philippines**on August 14, 2026 voiced strong support for the government’s push to integrate the National ID (PhilSys) into digital financial services across the Philippines. The alliance said the move will enable faster, more secure user verification for banks, e-wallets and online lenders, helping to reduce identity fraud and streamline Know-Your-Customer (KYC) processes. Officials believe PhilSys-linked KYC can lower onboarding friction for underserved customers and accelerate financial inclusion while strengthening anti-fraud measures nationwide. The group called for clear regulatory guidance, robust data privacy safeguards and phased implementation with industry partners to ensure a secure rollout.

Industry watchers say integrating PhilSys into digital KYC could significantly lower onboarding costs for fintechs and banks, speeding account opening for consumers and small businesses while reducing fraud losses. The move may expand reach to unbanked Filipinos, boost e-payments adoption and help SMEs access credit. Challenges remain, including data privacy, system interoperability and ensuring remote connectivity in rural areas; stakeholders urged coordination with the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas and the Philippine Statistics Authority to set technical standards, privacy safeguards and phased pilots to test security and uptake before nationwide rollout.
