The Philippine Statistics Authority has added three financial technology companies to its National ID Authentication Services, or NIDAS, as the government expands digital identity beyond card issuance. The new partners are Salmon Bank, ARX, and Island Credit Solution. Their integrations cover customer onboarding, business-to-business verification, and access to credit information. PSA says the goal is to make identity checks more secure and convenient while giving financial services a shared verification layer that can reduce repeated manual steps for customers today.

![PSA Expands National ID Fintech Links supporting image 1](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/national-id-fintech-links/image-1.webp?v=20261117080200)

The scale of the system is already significant. PSA Undersecretary Claire Dennis Mapa said 94,438,067 Filipinos were registered with the National ID, while more than 44 million financial-sector authentications had been performed as of August 31. Salmon Bank is using NIDAS for faster account opening and Know Your Customer processes. ARX Pay supports real-time business verification, while Island Credit Solution uses the service in its CreditMo platform for credit reports and related information across the country as more services move online.

![PSA Expands National ID Fintech Links supporting image 2](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/national-id-fintech-links/image-2.webp?v=20261117080200)

The expansion also arrives as the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas pushes regulated institutions toward stronger digital verification. PSA said a BSP memorandum covers customer verification, account opening, onboarding, and record updates, alongside data protection, cybersecurity, and anti-money-laundering safeguards. That combination gives NIDAS a more operational role in finance. It is not simply a government database; it is becoming infrastructure that private services can call when they need a trusted identity signal while preserving safeguards for privacy, security, and responsible financial access.

![PSA Expands National ID Fintech Links supporting image 3](https://viper.ph/api/media/articles/2026/11/national-id-fintech-links/image-3.webp?v=20261117080200)

For consumers, the practical test will be whether onboarding becomes faster without making privacy harder to understand. Financial institutions still need clear consent, secure handling, accurate records, and accessible alternatives when authentication fails. The new partnerships show how the Philippine National ID is moving toward everyday digital use, but trust will depend on implementation. Strong security controls and transparent explanations will matter as much as the convenience of completing more transactions online for Filipinos, banks, and regulated digital services nationwide.
