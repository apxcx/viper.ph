**Lenovo Legion**has partnered with Filipino toy designer Quiccs on a limited-edition collectible tied to Legion Gen 11 devices. YugaTech reports that the collaboration was unveiled at SuperManila Comic Con, combining Quiccs’ design language with Lenovo’s gaming identity and highlighting the growing link between local pop culture and gaming hardware.

The Lenovo Legion x Quiccs Promo 2026 runs from September 18 to October 31. Customers who purchase select participating Legion or LOQ devices from authorized stores may receive limited-edition toys, jerseys, or sticker packs, depending on the device. The freebie is provided upon purchase, with redemption limited to five times per customer under the promotion’s terms.
